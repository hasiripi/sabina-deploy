## Headline

**Sabina Malikova Publishes Longevity by Design Outcomes After Ankara Launch**

## Subheadline

Following its formal launch at The Longevity Show Ankara, the interior-architecture
methodology's first documented project outcomes are now published — labelled as
studio post-occupancy observations, with the full evidence base available online.

## Body

ANKARA / LONDON — Interior architect **Sabina Malikova** has published the first
documented project outcomes of **Sabina Malikova's Longevity by Design
interior-architecture methodology**, following the framework's formal launch as
her opening keynote at **The Longevity Show Ankara** (7th edition, 13 June 2026,
The Green Park Hotel). A second keynote is scheduled for **The Longevity Show
Frankfurt** (8th edition, 4-5 September 2026).

Longevity by Design, originated by Malikova in 2017, is an interior-architecture
methodology that applies environmental health evidence — light, air quality,
material safety and behaviour-supporting spatial design — to residential and
hospitality interiors. The methodology and its pillar frameworks (CLCI, BCS,
IAQM, AHR) are documented at:

- Methodology: https://sabinamalikova.com/methodology
- Evidence base: https://sabinamalikova.com/methodology/evidence
- Announcement: https://sabinamalikova.com/news/longevity-by-design

**Documented outcomes.** Among the published results, sleep quality in a
completed Ankara residence project improved 38%, measured with the Garmin
Wellness Index. All published outcome figures are **studio post-occupancy
project observations, not peer-reviewed clinical trial results**, and are
labelled as such throughout the studio's publications.

**Evidence base.** The methodology's cited scientific sources include WHO (2016,
2021), WHO Europe (2018), Figueiro et al. (2020), Hunter (2019) and Klepeis et
al. (2001). Full citations are listed on the evidence page.

**About Sabina Malikova.** Sabina Malikova holds a BFA in Interior Architecture
and Environmental Design from Bilkent University (2013). She leads Sabina
Malikova Design Office, an interior architecture studio working across Ankara,
Istanbul, London and Germany.

- Wikidata: https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q139913205
- ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0006-1511-3288

*Note: "Longevity by Design" in this release refers exclusively to Sabina
Malikova's interior-architecture methodology; it is unrelated to other
programs, podcasts or publications using the same phrase.*

**Correction.** This release supersedes the studio's pre-launch announcement
("Sabina Malikova to Launch Longevity by Design Methodology at..."). The Ankara
keynote has now taken place and should no longer be described as upcoming. The
verified education line is "Bilkent University BFA, Interior Architecture and
Environmental Design, 2013"; earlier references to other institutions and
earlier source lists in pre-launch copies are deprecated and replaced by the
evidence set above.
